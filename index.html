Things to Consider When Researching the Project

Whether evaluating a primary home or long-term residential property, homebuyers should conduct systematic due diligence. Key aspects to verify during your property research include:

  • MahaRERA Registration & Approvals: Verify the official MahaRERA project registration, sanctioned layout plans, building commencement certificates (CC), and declared completion timelines on the regulatory portal.
  • Unit Layouts & Usable Carpet Area: Review floor plans, room dimensions, RERA-defined carpet areas versus total area, natural lighting, and cross-ventilation.
  • Pricing & Cost Transparency: Understand the comprehensive cost structure, including base rates, floor rise charges, parking allotments, clubhouse memberships, statutory taxes (GST, stamp duty), and payment milestones.
  • Maintenance & Association Terms: Inquire about projected society maintenance charges, amenity management policies, water security provisions, and handover timelines.
  • Developer Track Record: Review the developer’s delivery history, construction quality standards, and overall performance on completed residential developments.
