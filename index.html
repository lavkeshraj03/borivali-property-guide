What Property Buyers Should Research

Whether considering a primary residence or a long-term asset, property researchers should execute a standardized due diligence process before making commitments. Key checklist items include:

  • RERA Registration & Compliance: Verify the project’s MahaRERA registration number, registered layout plans, declared completion dates, and any statutory disclosures uploaded to the official regulatory portal.
  • Title Clearances & Approvals: Confirm clear land title, Commencement Certificate (CC), Intimation of Disapproval (IOD), and environmental clearances.
  • Unit Layouts & Carpet Area: Review RERA carpet area measurements versus usable space, floor-to-ceiling heights, and natural ventilation layouts.
  • Financial Viability & Home Loan Approvals: Check pre-approval status with major public and private banking institutions, which conduct independent legal verification.
  • Maintenance & Association Terms: Understand expected society maintenance costs, parking allocation policies, and common area amenity handover schedules.
